Both legs move together in the goblet squat and Romanian deadlift. Both arms press together in the floor press. Each row round contains a left set then a right set; the repetitions are **per side**, not divided between sides. No tempo manipulation, drop sets, extra sets or unlisted circuit is required.

All external loads are **athlete-selected targets, not supplied numbers**. Record one dumbbell's mass for goblet squats and rows; record mass **per dumbbell** for the two-dumbbell Romanian deadlift and floor press. Do not silently interpret per-hand mass as combined mass or transfer a floor-press load from bench-press history.

Use the easy rehearsal sets to select an initial working resistance. Stop at the upper repetition target even if more repetitions seem possible. Stop sooner if the intended effort limit or movement control is reached; record the actual count, including a count below the lower target. If the chosen resistance clearly misses the intended effort, record it and adjust resistance for the next set within the available equipment; no fixed increment is prescribed. Do not force the repetition minimum or seek failure.

If 20 kg per dumbbell is too light, 2 kg is too heavy, or a 2-kg change prevents a suitable match, record the limitation and request review. Do not solve it by silently changing tempo, movement, repetitions beyond the range or set count. Pain or lost control ends the affected work; unresolved symptoms require clarification before resumption.

## Ordered preparation and transitions

This sequence applies to each session's actual exercise order; all rehearsal counts are separate from working sets.

1. Allow **3 minutes for initial setup**, including checking dumbbell locks, clearing the floor and opening the log.
2. Perform **3 minutes of comfortable walking or marching in place**, target RPE **2–3**. This is preparation, not a completed conditioning observation.
3. For the **first exercise**, perform **8 easy repetitions**, target RPE **3–4**, then rest **60 seconds**. Adjust to another still-easy rehearsal resistance, perform **5 repetitions**, target RPE **3–4**, then rest **90 seconds** before working set 1. Complete that exercise's working sets with the rests above.
4. Take **90 seconds of recovery/setup between exercises**, then rehearse the next exercise before its working sets. This transition is additional to, and precedes, that next exercise's rehearsal and post-rehearsal rest.
5. For the **second lower-body exercise**, perform **5 easy repetitions at RPE 3–4**, then rest **60 seconds** before working set 1.
6. For the **floor press**, perform **5 easy bilateral repetitions at RPE 3–4**, then rest **60 seconds** before working set 1.
7. For the **row**, rehearse **5 repetitions on the left**, target RPE **3–4**; allow **15 seconds to switch sides**; perform **5 on the right**, target RPE **3–4**; then rest **60 seconds** before the first working round.
8. Reserve **3 minutes total for logging**, distributed after sets and at the end. Record actual rest rather than assuming the target was followed. This allowance is additional to the stated recoveries; it is not permission to shorten them.

Steps 5–7 occur immediately before their respective exercises, in that day's table order. There are exactly **three** between-exercise transitions per session. No inter-set rest is charged after the final working set/round of an exercise; the separate transition handles recovery before the next rehearsal. In each row round, the 30-second side change still occurs before the right side.

Take additional recovery when needed to preserve intended effort and execution. The 40-minute fit is therefore **conditional**, not a cap on recovery. If extra recovery/setup uses the margin, omit the last listed upper-body exercise and record it as omitted; do not rush the leg work or disguise the omission as completion. If the priority leg work itself cannot fit with its recovery, stop at the slot boundary, record incomplete work and review the remaining prescription. This fallback is proposed for Greg's review too.
