## Session prescriptions

### D-7, Friday — speed and trap bar, about 43 minutes

1. Warm-up: 5 minutes easy bike; then A-march 2 × 15 m, A-skip 2 × 15 m,
   8 bodyweight hinges and 2 × 20 m gradual build-ups. Rest 30 seconds between
   drill trips/build-ups; allow 5 minutes for this block.
2. Two familiar countermovement jumps, **60 seconds rest**. Then **2 × 20 m
   accelerations at the familiar 85–90% perceived effort**, **3 minutes rest**.
   Take **3 minutes recovery** before the lifting ramp.
3. High-handle trap-bar ramp, all loads total: **135 × 5; rest 60 seconds;
   225 × 3; rest 90 seconds; 285 × 2; rest 3 minutes**.
4. Fixed monitoring: **335 × 2**, then **3 minutes rest**.
5. Working sets: **365 lb, 2 × 3**, target **RPE 7–8**, **3 minutes between sets**.
   Keep smooth movement; lower the working load if control cannot be maintained.
   Do not quietly change the monitoring load and retain the old protocol label.
6. After **2 minutes recovery**, dead bug **1 × 6 per side**, alternating with
   control. No between-set rest is needed for one set. Finish with a 3-minute walk.

Reduction versus the fixture's normal session: accelerations 4 to 2, jumps 3 to 2,
trap-bar working sets 3 to 2 and core sets 2 to 1. Monitoring remains one set;
it is counted separately and does not substitute for working volume.

### D-6, Saturday — bench and upper-body maintenance, about 38 minutes

1. Warm-up: 5 minutes easy bike, then **band pull-aparts 1 × 15 and scapular
   push-ups 1 × 8**, **30 seconds between drills**; allow 3 minutes for drills/setup.
2. Bench ramp: **45 × 10; rest 60 seconds; 95 × 5; rest 60 seconds;
   135 × 3; rest 90 seconds; 165 × 1; rest 3 minutes**.
3. Fixed monitoring: **185 × 2**, then **3 minutes rest**.
4. Bench working sets: **205 lb, 2 × 2**, target **RPE 7–8**,
   **3 minutes between sets**. Preserve smooth reps and log effort on each set.
5. After **3 minutes recovery**, strict pull-ups **1 × 6**, target **2 RIR**.
   Record fewer reps if the effort/control boundary arrives earlier.
6. After **2 minutes recovery**, dead bug **1 × 6 per side**. Finish with
   3 minutes easy walking. No additional bike block in this taper candidate.

Reduction: bench working sets 4 to 2, pull-up sets 3 to 1 and omission of the
usual 20–30-minute easy bike. That omission is a new taper proposal, not a reversal
of Greg's earlier acceptance of the full ordinary Saturday session.

### D-5, Sunday — existing rest day

### D-4, Monday — acceleration and squat, about 42 minutes

1. Use Friday's 10-minute general/dynamic preparation, with build-ups limited to
   the familiar controlled effort for this session.
2. **2 countermovement jumps**, **60 seconds rest**; then **2 × 10 m accelerations
   at 80–85% perceived effort**, **2 minutes between efforts**. Recover
   **3 minutes** before the squat ramp. The time budget includes extra setup margin.
3. Squat ramp: **45 × 8; rest 60 seconds; 135 × 5; rest 90 seconds;
   185 × 3; rest 90 seconds; 225 × 1; rest 3 minutes**.
4. Back squat **245 lb, 2 × 3**, target **RPE 6–7**, **3 minutes between sets**.
5. After **60 seconds recovery**, side plank **1 × 30 seconds per side**,
   **15 seconds to change sides**. Rest **60 seconds**, then bodyweight calf raise
   **1 × 12** with control. Finish with a 3-minute walk.

Reduction: acceleration efforts 4 to 2, jumps 3 to 2, squat sets 3 to 2 and each
accessory 2 to 1. Familiar load/effort is retained; no new overload or maximal test.

### D-3, Tuesday — steady state, 30 minutes total

**5 minutes walking/easy jogging**, then **20 minutes continuous easy running at
RPE 3–4**, then **5 minutes walking**. No interval rests; the main work is continuous.
The mode and duration are draft choices. Do not infer a pace from an RPE target.

### D-2, Wednesday — rest

No prescribed workout or make-up training.

### D-1, Thursday — shakeout, about 21 minutes

1. **5 minutes easy walk/jog**, comfortable effort, proposed RPE 2–3.
2. Familiar prehab: **bodyweight calf raises × 10, wall tibialis raises × 10,
   glute bridges × 8**; **30 seconds between drills**. One round, about 4 minutes.
3. Technique: **A-march 2 × 15 m**, **30 seconds between trips**;
   **bench 45 lb × 5**, then **60 seconds recovery** and **high-handle trap bar
   135 lb × 3**. Allow about 6 minutes including equipment changes. This is easy
   rehearsal, not a strength or stamina session; omit no required recovery to fit.
4. Dynamic mobility: controlled **leg swings 8 per side in each direction** and
   **arm circles 8 each direction**, **15–30 seconds between drills**; about
   4 minutes. Keep comfortable ranges. Allow 2 minutes for transitions/logging.

D-1 drills and intensity are new candidate choices; Greg approved their roles,
not these exact quantities. Familiarity is a fixture assumption, not an inferred
injury treatment requirement. Do not turn this into a conditioning circuit.
