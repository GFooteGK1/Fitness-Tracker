Strength effort: propose **2–3 reps in reserve**, recording actual reps and RPE
for each working set. Eight is a starting target, not a forced minimum; stop
earlier if the effort or movement-control boundary is reached. Do not infer
achieved reserve from the target, or automatically convert RPE to RIR. If even an
easier variation is unsuitable, clarify the limitation and revise that movement.

Rest initially 2–3 minutes between strength sets/rounds; take longer when needed
to maintain intended intensity. Timing below allows five minutes for each such
interval, but five minutes is not a recovery ceiling. Take needed recovery at
side changes and between exercises too. Avoid rushing to preserve a set count.

For bird dogs, alternate opposite arm/leg reaches from hands and knees. Maintain
trunk control and breathing, reducing reach if needed. Stop when control is lost;
this core prescription does not require approaching failure.

For rows, face the anchor, maintain a steady trunk, pull the chest toward the
handles, and lower under control. Technique reference:
[TRX's low-row instructions](https://www.trxtraining.com/blogs/news/7-basic-movements).
This reference supports the movement description, not this exact dose or week.
