## Working sets: effort governs repetitions

**End each working set with approximately 2 controlled repetitions in reserve (2 RIR).** The companion target is approximately **RPE 8 on a RIR-anchored resistance-training scale**, where 10 means no further controlled repetitions. This is a prescribed target, not a measured equivalence for an athlete's report. Record actual RPE and estimated RIR separately; do not derive or prefill one from the other.

Choose an appropriate load during rehearsal. Perform the repetitions needed to approach 2 RIR while maintaining control. **Do not stop at 12 merely because 12 was printed in the original draft if many more controlled repetitions remain.** Repetitions are an outcome of the selected resistance and response, not a mandatory cap or minimum. Lighter resistance may require more repetitions. Do not turn every set into a failure test.

## Prescriptive preparation and transitions

Preparation stays easy and is separate from working volume; **do not take rehearsal sets to 2 RIR**.

1. Initial setup: 3 minutes to check locks, clear the floor and open the log.
2. Walk or march comfortably for 3 minutes at RPE 2–3.
3. First exercise: 8 easy reps at RPE 3–4, rest 60 s; adjust to another still-easy rehearsal load, perform 5 reps at RPE 3–4, then rest 90 s before working sets.
4. Between exercises: 90 s for recovery and setup, additional to the next exercise's rehearsal and rest. There are three such transitions if all four exercises are completed.
5. Before the other lower-body exercise: 5 easy reps at RPE 3–4, then 60 s rest.
6. Before floor press: 5 easy bilateral reps at RPE 3–4, then 60 s rest.
7. Before rows: 5 easy left reps at RPE 3–4, 15 s to switch, 5 easy right reps at RPE 3–4, then 60 s rest.
8. Reserve 3 minutes total for logging, distributed through the session, additional to prescribed rests.

Perform these rehearsals in the day's exercise order. There is no extra inter-set rest after the final set/round; the transition supplies recovery before the next exercise's rehearsal. Both row rounds still include the between-side recovery. Extend recovery when needed to maintain execution and intended effort.
