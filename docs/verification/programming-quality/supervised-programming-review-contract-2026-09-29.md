Use the following distinctions throughout review:

| Evidence level | What can be carried forward | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accepted general principle | A reasoning requirement, with its stated conditions | A universal exercise, dose, threshold or automatic change |
| Accepted case decision | The chosen response to the facts actually presented | Applicability to materially different athletes or circumstances |
| Accepted complete example | The exact reviewed session/week and its assumptions | A personal prescription, future performance, compiler correctness or unseen quality |
| Verified implementation | The behavior demonstrated by the named test/artifact | Human coaching acceptance or hosted availability |

## Review surface for the supervised cycle

The following consolidates [policy 0.2](../../coach/initial-dose-policy-0.2-review.md), rules 1–9, with the [accepted milestone](../../plans/programming-readiness-assessment-2026-09-29.md). It is the required information surface, not evidence that a pilot is ready:

- Identify the athlete, accepted base/version, confirmed goal, current constraints and source dates/revisions. Label observed, reported, estimated, proposed and unknown values.
- Present the complete proposed week. Show its preparation, working/monitoring roles, effort, rest, load conventions, side counts, conditional work and honest timing. Explain each change and what response will inform the next decision.
- Distinguish unchanged reviewed content, an applicable reviewed operation and a new prescription needing coach review. Material unsupported changes return for a focused review; they must not acquire hidden defaults.
- Preserve coach judgment separately from athlete acceptance. Bind accepted changes to the current source and new version. A stale source correction requires renewed review of the affected proposal.
- After training, compare prescribed and actual work, corrections, omissions, set effort, symptoms and context. Record what happened before proposing another change; successful persistence alone does not validate its coaching quality.
